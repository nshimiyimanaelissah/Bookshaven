How it works

  1. Choose your role at login: Admin, Teacher, or Student.
  2. Strong passwords required: at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character.
  3. Teachers and students create accounts with email, name, password. Students also enter level, class, and section.
  4. Admins log in with email or username, password, and their answer word.
  5. Browse the library — free documents (Books of RE) need no payment.
  6. Submit your payment record with the reference from your SMS, then the admin verifies it.
Email verification and password reset in this file are simulated. Real email delivery needs an SMTP server (use the Express version).
