How it works

BookHaven uses administrator-reviewed payment references in this prototype. Real online payments and cross-device accounts require a secure server connection.

  1. Choose your role at login: Teacher or Student.
  2. New passwords must score at least 50% on the password-strength checker.
  3. Teachers and students create accounts with username. Students also enter level, class, and section.
  4. Browse the library and filter by level, class, and section (pathway) to find the right documents.
  5. Select a book to see its details and choose View or Download.
  6. For paid downloads, follow the configured MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or card payment instructions, then submit the transaction reference. Access stays locked until an administrator verifies and approves the payment.
  7. After approval, you can read the book for 30 days and download it once.
